Notifications are coming to Pro. They are not available yet.

## What it will do

When a session raises something that needs review, the alert reaches you where you already are,
and you approve or deny from the alert itself - without going back to the terminal or the
dashboard.

## Where

Telegram, Slack and more.

## How it gets set up

You connect your account from the grith.ai dashboard, in a couple of clicks. grith runs the
integration; there is nothing to configure locally.

## The command today

`grith notifications` already exists and says the same thing:

<Terminal>
{`$ grith notifications status
Notifications are coming soon.

Get alerts — and approve or deny — from Telegram, Slack and more,
set up from your dashboard with no local configuration. It's on the
way; this command will light up when it arrives.`}
</Terminal>
